I study the drivers of technological change and its impacts on the broader economy.

I am a Tenure-track Assistant Professor (RTD-B) of Economic Policy at the Department of Economics and Statistics “Cognetti de Martiis”, University of Turin, and a Fellow of BRICK at Collegio Carlo Alberto.

My current work covers green and circular-economy innovation, the direction of technological change and the rise of intangible assets, patents and intellectual property, critical raw materials, and the labor-market effects of technological change. My research has appeared in Research Policy, Economics of Innovation and New Technology, Small Business Economics, Industrial and Corporate Change, Environmental and Resource Economics, Regional Studies, Eurasian Business Review, Industry and Innovation, The Journal of Technology Transfer, and Journal of Evolutionary Economics.

I received my PhD in Economics from the Vilfredo Pareto Doctorate (University of Turin and Collegio Carlo Alberto) in 2018, and was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Bordeaux before joining the Department as an Assistant Professor in 2020. I currently serve as the Department’s Deputy Director for Third Mission and Public Engagement.
